# Mogul Clip privacy policy

Mogul Clip is a Chrome extension that saves the page you're on to your own Mogul account, as a note.

## What it reads

Only when you click the extension's button, and only on the tab you clicked it on, Mogul Clip reads:

- the page's title,
- the page's address (URL),
- any text you have selected on the page.

It does not read pages in the background, track your browsing, or read any other tab.

## Where it goes

When you save, the title, URL, selected text, and anything you typed are sent to your Mogul account at `https://studio.mogul.tools`, and nowhere else.
The extension also asks your Mogul account whether you already have a note for the page, by sending the page's URL, so that it can show you that note instead of starting a new one.

Requests are authenticated by the session you already have with Mogul in the same browser.
The extension does not read, store, or send your password or your cookies itself.

## What it keeps in the browser

Text you have typed but not yet saved, so that it isn't lost when the popup closes.
It is kept per page until you save, and is cleared when Chrome closes.

## What it doesn't do

- It does not sell or share your data with anyone.
- It does not use your data for advertising, for creditworthiness or lending, or for anything other than saving your notes.
- It contains no analytics or tracking, and loads no code from anywhere else.

## Your notes

Your notes live in your Mogul account.
You can edit or delete them at any time in the Mogul studio, and they are kept until you delete them.
